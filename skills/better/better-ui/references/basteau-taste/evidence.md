# Evidence behind the taste

What each source teaches, reduced to transferable moves. Use it to calibrate a
decision, not as a catalogue to copy: no site's signature should reappear elsewhere.

## Own sites

### basteau.exe.xyz (personal site)

- Identity on the left, a playable board on the right. The board is a memory game
  of the tools the owner works with, so play is about the subject.
- White ground, near-black ink, one muted gray, rules as ink at 12-16% alpha;
  a tested dark theme that follows the system unless the visitor picks the other.
- One family in two roles: Geist at 800 (display, `-.055em`, line-height `.85`) and
  300 (tagline); Geist Mono 500 for the tracked uppercase eyebrow and the score.
- Signature from the name: the last three letters ("eau") hold animated water that
  rises with game progress and splashes on tap. The 404 page reuses it, drained.
- Logo colors (magenta, cyan, orange, violet) live on the tiles; the interface
  chrome stays ink. Tiles carry print grain and a riso-style off-register ghost.
- Theme switch is the one occasional "moment": a soft-edged circle reveal grown
  from the button. Everything else is 150-450ms.
- Sound belongs to the game (tile pitch from color, timbre from shape, a
  pentatonic climb per match) with a visible mute, off for reduced motion.

### sebdev.be (studio site)

- Dark ink ground, bone text, pebble muted; one magenta call to action. Field tiles
  in magenta, cyan and orange frame an empty middle.
- Locked copy of four parts: eyebrow, wordmark, one-line promise, one CTA. A second
  plain email link was removed as a duplicate.
- Wordmark: a hairline magenta and cyan `text-shadow` split that widens on hover via
  `@property`, plus a one-time flip of the mirrored "bd" pair after load. A cursor
  glow, a foil glint and pointer-tracked JS versions were rejected for CSS.
- The bland middle got a faint dot grid that swells near the cursor and lights with
  click ripples, kept clear of the copy (a dot beside the tagline read as a bullet).
  Random twinkle and colored dots were rejected to protect the single accent.
- Sound only on two deliberate clicks (the CTA chime, the wordmark flip). A pop on
  background clicks, per-tile ticks, hover sounds and a success chord were removed.
- A 2x2 tile mark drives favicon, cards and QR badge from one generator. The two
  sites credit each other in the eyebrow with a mirrored hover.

## Inspiration

### savor.it (food brand)

- Cinematic, close-up photography of process and material does the talking.
- Condensed light display serif for headlines, a neutral grotesk for small
  captions, mono for labels. Display type is huge; captions are tiny.
- Warm cream and deep maroon instead of white and black.
- Scroll storytelling: thin hairline paths connect short captions over full-bleed
  textures; copy arrives a phrase at a time.
- Lesson: for a small food or maker brand, the product image is the gesture, and a
  warm brand-derived neutral pair replaces the default ink and white.

### public.joannabui.com (designer portfolio)

- Soft off-white ground, one rounded image window with a single orange shape.
- The intro is one sentence with the name set in a handwritten script and tiny
  inline icons; a second face used for exactly one word.
- Small uppercase tabs, a grid of work cards, a playful intro animation of colored
  circles, crisp custom easing with occasional overshoot.
- A human closing line in the footer.
- Lesson: personality can live inside a sentence; one expressive word beats a
  decorated layout.

### ped.ro (personal site)

- Dark grain ground; the biography is one long paragraph, blurred and dimmed until
  attention brings parts into focus.
- Key nouns become uppercase outlined pills inline in the prose; one is filled in
  the accent.
- Small serif navigation with a thin accent underline on the current page; a small
  counter invites discovery.
- Lesson: reward curiosity with progressive reveal; let inline tags carry hierarchy
  inside prose.

### raphaelsalaja.com (design engineer site)

- One narrow column of small, calm type; links in muted gray instead of color.
- Work as a grid of thumbnails with hairline frames; writing as rows with title
  left and category and date right in muted tabular figures.
- A faded top edge and a small floating pill toolbar; expressive faces are reserved
  for the experiments, not the shell.
- Lesson: restraint in the frame lets crafted pieces stand out; density can be high
  when hierarchy is quiet and aligned.

## Shared threads

Across all six: one focal idea per view, neutral or brand-warm ground, a single
accent, size and weight extremes in type, human copy, small crafted interactions
that respond to attention, and texture or photography instead of decoration.
